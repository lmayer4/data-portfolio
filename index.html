About

I come from a biology and veterinary background, and I am training in data science to work on health and clinical questions. My clinical experience as a veterinary technician taught me how records are kept and why accuracy is important, and my graduate work focuses on applying statistics and programming to biological and health data.

Projects

Maternal depression and household income (NHANES)

Analysis of NHANES data on women with children 18 years or younger, examining associations between depression status and household income, marital status, miscarriages, and current pregnancy.

R, data cleaning, t-tests, chi-square and Fisher tests

Self-rated health and lifetime hypertension (MEPS)

Cross-sectional analysis of Medical Expenditure Panel Survey data on whether poor or fair self-rated health is associated with a lifetime hypertension diagnosis, stratified by household income.

R, prevalence ratios, odds ratios, prevalence differences

Skills

Programming and tools: R, Python, SQL, JMP, Microsoft Excel

Methods: Hypothesis testing (t-tests, chi-square, Fisher exact), prevalence and odds ratios, regression, measures of association, data cleaning and validation

Data sources: NHANES, MEPS
